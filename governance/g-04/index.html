3Roles and Responsibilities

Each role in the LinkScape organization carries specific responsibilities:

RoleKey Responsibilities
CEO & Co-FounderOrganizational vision and strategic direction; final decision authority; external relations and partnerships; Founder/Co-founder veto per Ordinance s.13.
Co-Founder & CFOFinancial stewardship; Hack Club Bank relationship and reporting; budgeting, expense approval and compliance calendar oversight.
CTOTechnical direction and architecture decisions; compute resource governance (80x H100 fleet); platform security and access control.
Project MaintainersProject-specific decisions, code review, and team coordination.
MembersContributions, issue resolution, and community participation.
FellowsAlumni and senior contributors providing mentorship, technical review, and advisory input. Non-fiduciary, no operational authority.
